        What “correction” means here
        

          The decoder returns graph edges and a logical-Z flip prediction. The
          qubit overlay is a minimum-weight Pauli representative with the same
          detector signature and predicted observable, found from Stim-simulated
          faults; it is not a gate-by-gate translation of those edges.
          Verification replays that representative at the original fault
          location. This is a counterfactual Pauli-frame demonstration, not
          causal real-time feedback or board execution. A zero syndrome does not
          guarantee logical success, and Z-memory success does not certify an
          arbitrary quantum state.
